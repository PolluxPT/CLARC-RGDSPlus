CLARC RGDS+ v1.0.0

Initial public standalone release for the Anbernic RGDS+ stock Linux environment.

Download CLARC_RGDSPlus_v1.0.0.zip and copy its APPS folder to your SD card.
Copy your own DRM-free Linux archive, clarc_1.0.0.3.tar.gz, into
APPS/CLARC_RGDSPlus/. Launch CLARC; the first launch prepares it automatically.

Controls: D-pad/left stick move; L1/R1 rotate; START opens pause/menu;
hold SELECT+START for 1.5 seconds to exit. Physical volume controls, the
volume curve and restoration, and bottom-screen shutdown are included.

Game files are not included. Only the specified Linux release is supported.
The owner has confirmed gameplay and the corrected controls on the RGDS+.
